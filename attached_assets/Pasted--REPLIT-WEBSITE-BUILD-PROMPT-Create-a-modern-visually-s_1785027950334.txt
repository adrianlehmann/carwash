# REPLIT WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT

Create a modern, visually stunning, SEO-optimized website for **Crystal Clean Car Wash**, a locally trusted, 24-hour car wash in Joplin, Missouri. The website should immediately communicate convenience, quality, affordability, and exceptional cleaning results.

The design should feel bright, fresh, and modern with clean layouts, smooth animations, high-quality vehicle photography, and strong calls-to-action.

The goal is to encourage visitors to choose Crystal Clean Car Wash over competing automatic car washes by highlighting the quality of the wash, spotless results, affordable pricing, and well-maintained equipment.
